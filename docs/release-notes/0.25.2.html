What's new in 0.25.2

A fix for an update that could not finish. If 0.25.1 stopped partway through moving your library and asked you to quit and try again, this release lets it finish. Your library was never changed.

The update could stop on a shared attachment

  • Copying a conversation that contains an image made the update refuse to run. The copy points at the same image file as the original, and Mechanician treated that as a reason to stop rather than something ordinary. One copied conversation was enough to hold up the whole library, on every attempt.
  • It is now recorded and carried across. The image stays exactly where it is, both conversations still show it, and the update continues.
  • The same thing prevented that conversation from saving after a successful update. That is fixed too.

When an update cannot finish, it now says why

  • The message names what stopped it instead of restating the rule it was checking. The old wording could not be acted on, by you or by us, and the only machine with the answer was the one that could not start.

Launch timing

  • Settings, under Advanced, now breaks the wait for your conversation into the work it is made of, rather than reporting it as one number.
