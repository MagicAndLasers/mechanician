What's new in 0.25.1

Fixes for things 0.25.0's move to a database left behind. There is no migration in this update and nothing changes about your library.

Saving

  • A conversation could stop saving and keep failing. If a message appeared and was withdrawn at the wrong moment, such as a streamed reply replaced by a final one, the record it left behind blocked every later save of that conversation. The banner said your messages were not on disk, and it never cleared. Nothing was lost, because the save was refused rather than half-written, but the conversation could not be saved again until you restarted.

Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight

  • They can see your whole library again. They were still reading the copy your library was migrated from, so a conversation or artifact created since the update could not be picked in Shortcuts, opened by Siri, or found in Spotlight, and one you had deleted still turned up.
  • Spotlight rebuilds its list of artifacts at every launch. It no longer empties that list when it cannot read the source.

Recovering a conversation Mechanician could not read

  • Settings can list them again. After the update the recovery list came up empty with an error, so a conversation kept there could not be reached at all.
  • Restoring one now sticks. It used to reappear for the rest of the session and be gone at the next launch, and trying a second time failed for good. The exact original file is still kept, and you can still reveal or save a copy of it instead.

Scheduled tasks

  • A scheduled task runs in the right folder with the right instructions. It was reading workspace settings as they stood before the update: a workspace you edited since ran with its old folder, one you created since was not found at all and ran from your home folder, and one you deleted still ran.

Reclaiming the space from the migration

  • 0.25.0 said your previous library's space would come back after a week. It could not. The check for a verified backup looked in the wrong place and always failed, so nothing was ever released. It works now, and when it runs Mechanician tells you what it released and how much came back.
  • It releases only the rollback copy. The files your library was migrated from stay, because a conversation Mechanician could not read has its only remaining copy among them.

Messages

  • Errors say what happened to your work instead of repeating the storage engine's own words. If a conversation cannot be saved, the message now says your messages are on screen but not yet on disk, and the technical detail goes to the log where support can read it.
  • The same pass covered dictation, connection settings, and Keychain messages, and styled three buttons that had been left with the stock system appearance.
