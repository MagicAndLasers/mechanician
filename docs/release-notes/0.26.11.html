What's new in 0.26.11

A change your organization publishes now takes effect even if you had edited that setting on your own Mac. This affects organizations that use a managed configuration; everything else is unchanged.

Your local edits could outrank your organization, permanently

  • Editing the model list in Managed Configuration replaced the published list for good. A model your organization later withdrew kept being offered, and a model they added never appeared, no matter how many times the configuration updated.
  • This was hard to spot because the app was right about everything else. Settings correctly reported the new revision, and the update had genuinely arrived. Only the list itself was still the old one, and nothing said so.
  • Your edits are still yours. They are kept for as long as your organization leaves that setting alone, which is the reason to make one: your deployment may not carry a model the profile names. When your organization changes the same setting, theirs is used again, and the app tells you which of your edits were replaced and by which revision.
  • This applies to the model list, the project, the region, and a source's address. Turning a managed source off stays your choice, because a published update says nothing about it.
  • The model picker now says when the list you are looking at is a local edit rather than the one your organization published.
  • If a Mac is already in this state, open Settings, choose Show Configuration, then Reset All to Profile and Save. Quit and reopen to finish.
