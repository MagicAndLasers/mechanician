What's new in 0.26.31

This Daily build gives Mechanician a memory. It keeps a wiki about you and your work, built from what you have already said, and every statement in it carries the words it came from.

  • Memory is a new window, and a tab beside your conversation. It holds a page for you, a page for each project you work on, and a page for the people and companies you mention. Open it from the toolbar, or from Window then Memory.
  • Build my profile reads the conversations you already have and proposes a first set of statements. Nothing is remembered until you accept it, and a review inbox shows what is waiting.
  • Every statement records the words you actually said and the conversation they were said in. A page reads as an article: a summary at the top, statements grouped by what they are about, and numbered references you can follow to the quote behind each one.
  • The assistant brings what it knows about you into a conversation, on every provider, and tells you when it has. It can also offer to remember something you have just said, for you to accept or reject.
  • Asking is how you change the wiki. You correct, merge and remove statements by saying so in the Memory workspace. The assistant does not decide on its own what is true about you.
  • A fact said twice stops becoming two statements, and removing something keeps it removed even if it is later said again in different words.
  • The assistant can look up public context about a subject, such as a company you work for, and brings back what it finds as proposals for you to review rather than as facts. That research runs with no access to your repository, your local tools, or your instructions.
  • Pages open without the hundreds of database queries earlier builds made, and grouping a long page is far faster.
  • Fixes: the selected conversation's title is readable again in the sidebar, a toolbar button recovered its tooltip, and following a citation in a summary now moves to the reference it names.
