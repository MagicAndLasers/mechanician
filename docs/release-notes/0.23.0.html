What's new in 0.23.0

Open local files your way

  • Click a local file link to preview it in Mechanician. The file opens in a resizable window with its complete location visible and selectable.
  • Right-click the link to choose what happens: Preview in Mechanician, Open in Default App, Reveal in Finder, or Copy Path. The preview window offers the same external actions.
  • Source references such as RootView.swift:120 resolve to the underlying file. Binary and large files open in their normal app instead of being forced into a text preview.

Find that stays found

  • The current Find result remains visibly highlighted while you move through a long conversation, including results that begin offscreen or in rows the transcript reuses.
  • Your messages highlight the exact matching words instead of coloring the whole message.

Workspaces stay in their lane

  • The conversation sidebar now belongs strictly to the current workspace. Its rows, count, and search can no longer turn into a hidden folder switcher.
  • Opening, moving, forking, restoring, or starting a conversation now keeps the workspace's identity intact, including during late startup work and after a workspace is removed.
