What's new in 0.18.0

Mail arrives as a real message

  • Drop messages directly from Apple Mail. Mechanician saves the original message in the conversation, identifies it clearly as mail, and gives the agent readable message content instead of only the subject.
  • Read mail in the transcript. Expand a message card to see its sender, date, and readable body, or open the original message for its complete MIME content.

Provider recovery finishes the handoff

  • Reconnect without another model-picker step. From a conversation that needs a connection, choose, connect, or reconnect an account in Providers. Once verified, Mechanician applies that route and its default model to the originating conversation while preserving its history and draft.
  • Come back to a ready conversation. Connect and Reconnect return focus to Mechanician after verification, and failed recovery restores an actionable connection card instead of leaving an endless progress state.

Light Mode stays vivid, legible, and compact

  • Selection and status remain unmistakable. Conversations keep a clear blue selection, while Help, marketplace details, badges, and activity labels retain readable contrast on light surfaces.
  • Activity graphics keep all of their information. The compact three-color spinner loses its white backplate, timelines stay inside their panel, and token history uses quiet blue and gray instead of beige or gold.
  • Narrow windows protect the composer. Controls compact cleanly and the inspector yields space before the command area becomes unusable.
