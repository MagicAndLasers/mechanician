What's new in 0.21.0

Undo

  • ⌘Z puts back anything Mechanician removed or moved, and never touches what an agent did. Move a conversation or an artifact to another workspace, delete a conversation, delete every conversation, delete an artifact — all of it comes back.
  • A whole multi-selection undoes as one step. Moving twenty conversations is one ⌘Z, not twenty.
  • Deleting still asks first. The confirmation stops the accident; undo covers the times you confirmed on autopilot.
  • A restored conversation is exactly what you had — its transcript, its pasted images, its attached files, and its queued prompts still paused, so nothing is sent on your behalf when you open it again.
  • While you're typing, ⌘Z still undoes your typing.

Find in a conversation

  • ⌘F searches the conversation you're reading, with a count of where you are: "3 of 17". ⌘G and ⇧⌘G step through matches, and ⌘E searches for whatever you've selected.
  • A match inside a collapsed tool group opens it rather than being skipped.
  • Searching your list of conversations moved to ⌥⌘F.

Send things to Mechanician from anywhere

  • Services. Select text in any app and use its Services menu: start a new conversation with that text, or add it to the conversation you already have open. The second one leaves Mechanician in the background, so you can clip several passages while reading without being pulled out of Safari each time.
  • In Finder, open the selected folders as workspaces, or attach the selected files to a conversation.
  • Links. mechanician:// opens a conversation, a workspace, or an artifact from a note, a script, or a Shortcut. Copy Link is on the Conversation menu, the sidebar, the artifact list, and the workspace cards.
  • None of it ever sends. Text and files land in the composer and stop there, for you to read before you press Return.

Your windows come back

  • Quitting and reopening restores the whole session, not one window. Every workspace window returns, tab groups come back whole and in order, and focus lands on the window and tab you were last in.
  • A workspace you deleted in the meantime isn't reopened, and a deleted conversation leaves its window on that workspace rather than taking the window with it.

Fixes

  • Opening two conversations in quick succession no longer leaves one window blank.
  • Revealing an artifact works on a cold launch, and can no longer open a second Artifacts window.
  • Text pasted from another app can't smuggle in a file attachment you didn't choose.
  • Tool labels on the orange ray are legible again.
  • Confirmation sheets no longer claim deleting can't be undone, because now it can.
