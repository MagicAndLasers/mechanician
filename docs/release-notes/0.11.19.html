What’s new in 0.11.19

  • Claude 5 in the model picker. Updates the Claude Agent SDK and exposes the current Opus 5, Sonnet 5, and Fable 5 models with explicit generation labels, correct 1M context windows, and selections that survive a cold launch.
  • A calmer command box. The activity outline is rebuilt around three continuous motions instead of staged phases: the palette drifts, two long feathered arcs of light drift at a different rate, and the whole outline breathes. Nothing restarts or snaps, and the chromatic keyline stays unbroken all the way round.
  • A cursor that belongs to the app. The insertion point takes its colour from the same palette the outline drifts through, carries a soft phosphor glow, and dims while a turn is running so you can see at a glance that typing will queue.
  • The composer takes focus when it should. Switching conversations — from the sidebar, Spotlight, a background turn, or after deleting one — now leaves you ready to type, as does launching the app or opening a window.
  • A native delivery menu. The send-mode chooser is now a standard macOS menu, which also removes a crash that could occur when it opened.
