Runtime reliability architecture

  • Keeps an older compatible Runtime Service fully usable until it explicitly proves durable upgrade-drain support and reaches a safe handoff checkpoint.
  • Accepts new conversation turns durably through Runtime Service reconnects and upgrades instead of disabling Enter when the live transport flickers.
  • Keeps model choices stable through reconnects while binding every cached catalog to the exact Claude or Codex account that supplied it.
  • Prevents parallel builds from replacing a candidate and blocks publication until the exact installed helper authenticates, discovers models, and completes real Claude and Codex turns.
