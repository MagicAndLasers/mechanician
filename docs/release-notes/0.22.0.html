What's new in 0.22.0

Print a conversation

  • ⌘P prints the conversation you're reading. The title goes at the top, and the standard Mac header and footer carry the job name, the date, and "Page 2 of 12" — so a printed transcript is dated and paginated without you setting anything up.
  • The page is always light with black ink, whatever appearance the app is in. Rendering for paper is not rendering for a screen.
  • What reaches the page is what was said: your messages and the replies. Tool calls print as one compact line each rather than dumping their output, and anything the model withdrew never prints at all — the same rule that keeps it out of Copy Transcript.
  • Print, or use the PDF button in the print panel to keep a copy.

Fixes

  • Save as PDF no longer comes out dark. Exporting an artifact while the app was in Dark Mode produced light text on a dark background — a page of solid ink on paper. Exports are always light now, while the preview panels still follow the app.
  • The agent activity chart stays inside its panel. Cards no longer overflow at wide or narrow inspector widths, the live edge follows the pixels that actually mean "now" instead of stopping short of them, and a short running turn no longer stretches to fill the viewport.

Known limitations

  • Printing does not include images from a conversation yet.
