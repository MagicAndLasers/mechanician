What's new in 0.26.12

Reasoning levels now come from your model provider rather than from a list baked into the app.

Ultra works on Claude through Vertex AI and Bedrock

  • Ultra was only offered on Claude accounts and the Anthropic API. On a managed Claude route it was missing, with nothing explaining why. That was an oversight: everything underneath already supported it.
  • It now appears on every Claude route whose model offers Extra High, which is what it has always been built on.

New reasoning levels appear when your provider adds them

  • A reasoning level added by Anthropic or OpenAI after a given Mechanician release was invisible, and could not be selected, until Mechanician itself updated. The app checked every level against a fixed list and quietly discarded anything it did not recognize.
  • Levels the app knows are still shown cheapest to strongest. Anything new is listed after them, and says plainly that this version cannot say how it compares, rather than implying a position it cannot justify.
  • For managed setups this also removes a sharp edge: a configuration naming a reasoning level newer than the app was rejected in full, taking every model and provider in it down. Only a malformed entry is refused now.
