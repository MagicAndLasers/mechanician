What's new in 0.26.6

Checking a managed configuration for updates now says you are up to date when you are. This affects organizations that use a managed configuration; everything else is unchanged.

Check for Updates reported a difference that was not there

  • On a Mac where any part of the managed configuration had been changed locally, every check reported that your organization had published something. It had not. The difference being detected was your own local change.
  • Mechanician was comparing what your organization publishes against your configuration with your local edits already applied. Those two can never match once you have edited anything, so the check could never come out clean.
  • It now compares against your configuration as your organization signed it, which is what the feed actually serves. A machine with local edits can report being up to date again, and a real change from your organization is still detected and installed exactly as before.

This matters more after 0.26.5, which rewrote that message to name a cause. On an affected Mac it would have told you to ask your administrator to republish something they had already published correctly.
